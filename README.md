# My Favorite Series: Hidden Love

**Hidden Love** is a popular 2023 Chinese drama about **Sang Zhi**, a student who has a secret crush on her older brother's college friend, **Duan Jiaxu**. After losing touch, they meet again when she starts university and slowly fall in love.


## Main Characters
1. **Sang Zhi** – Played by *Zhao Lusi*
2. **Duan Jiaxu** – Played by *Chen Zheyuan*
3. **Sang Yan** – Played by *Victor Ma*

> *"Study hard and get into the same university so we can meet again."*
